# record-integrity — _global

A rules file: plans are editable until they become records; records are annotated, never rewritten.

## Job

Let the person change anything that is still a plan, keep every record of what happened, and leave a mark where a record was touched, so the Review's number can always be checked against the day.

## Layout and components

No surface of its own. The item sheet, the day header sheet, the Adjust sheet, the Review screens and the Settings screens carry these rules.

## Words

_Archive_ never _Delete_, except the account. _Undo_ on every toast. _Not assigned today_ for a trim, _cut when shifted_ for a cut, _moved_ for a late start; never _failed_, _skipped_ or _overdue_.

## Access

Every undo is a toast action with a keyboard-reachable button inside the four-second toast; the record's own control is the durable path. Absent controls are absent, not disabled, so nothing announces as unavailable.
