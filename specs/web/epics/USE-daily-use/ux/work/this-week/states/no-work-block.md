# This week on the phone — a day with no Work block

Saturday 17 October, say, built from the usual week with no Work block. `?state=no-work-block` [ASSUMPTION: key].

## What shows, top to bottom

[ASSUMPTION] The day as Set my days draws it, with no tiers and no fit line. **Pool** stays at the foot (the pool is the week's, not the day's). The place sheet's Days greys a day with no Work block, with the words [PROPOSED, to Gloss] "No Work block" in place of its fit, and its action waits for another day; the desk's Week reads the same day "No work blocks" ([`../../week/words-and-access.md`](../../week/words-and-access.md)). A placing onto it is never refused silently: the day says why ([`../../../interactions/move-to/states/no-block-of-its-kind.md`](../../../interactions/move-to/states/no-block-of-its-kind.md) is the Item sheet's same case).

## What the person can do

Open Pool and place onto another day; add a Work block for the day with the + (the New block sheet, Work kind); move on with the chips.

## Rulings

- D-USE-54: Place opens a sheet with Days, Tier and the fit per day.
- D-USE-57: work lives in Work blocks' tiers.

## Widths

Not drawn. 1024 and up: the Week's "No work blocks" day foot.
