## What the person can do

- **Work left:** − + or type; the preview and sum change with it [ASSUMPTION: half-hour steps, as Size and length's hours].
- **Which block:** Morning, Afternoon, or Pick blocks for any mix (not drawn open) ([`../../index.md`](../../index.md) step 2a).
- **Days:** toggle a day; each picked day adds a row to the preview.
- **Each session:** the whole block, or a set length [ASSUMPTION: a length stepper replaces the words; not drawn].
- **Tier:** one tier for every session [ASSUMPTION: a `Select` of Urgent, Primary, Secondary, Stretch].
- **Carry on next week:** on or off.
- **Add 4 sessions:** places them ([`../../index.md`](../../index.md) step 3). **Cancel**, ×, Esc or a click on the scrim: closes with nothing placed [ASSUMPTION for the scrim].

## The notes on the boards

**week / spread a big task / 1728 / light** (Note · week / spread):

> week / spread a big task / 1728 / light
>
> A big task (SYN-218, 20 h) spread across mornings: "Spread…" on the pool row, or on a session's menu. Choose the hours left, which Work block (Morning, Afternoon, or Pick blocks for any mix), the days, the session length (the whole block, or a set length), and the tier. The preview shows each session with what it shares the block with and how long it gets once the block's other work is counted; afternoons stay free for changing work. "Carry on next week" keeps placing sessions until the hours are done. Sessions are one item: checking a session is "Done for today", and every session shows n of N. Editing one session later asks This session or All sessions [PROPOSED].

## Rulings

- D-USE-42: Spread sets hours left, block, days, session length and tier, previews the sessions, and carries on next week [PROPOSED]. D-USE-40: one item as sessions.
- D-USE-54, D-USE-57: the phone's Spread is a full sheet with these fields and a Days and tier sub-page.
- [ASSUMPTION] Monday is dashed because it is today and its Morning Work is under way; a day whose chosen block has begun is not offered.
- [ASSUMPTION] The sample is off by one session: the background's Tue Primary holds [Alder intro] 2 of 3, yet the Tue row shares nothing. The rule (each row names what it shares and the time left) wins over the sample.
