- **Life and To-dos, framing round 4 (Taylor, 2026-10-10).** [PROPOSED] Type belongs to the to-do only; a block never filters by it. A To-dos block fills from to-dos with no day, oldest first, of any type; Taylor picks or drops any on the day, and the From To-dos sheet keeps a type filter (All types by default) to help picking. "Errands" and "Sunday reset" are block names, not rules. Supersedes round 3's per-block type picker. Awaiting Taylor. Carried in part (the To-dos block in go mode): `day/now/states/todos-block.md`. Carried (the block half, the Type filter on a To-dos block's picker): `day/block-view/states/todos-pool.md`, `day/block-view/states/todos-add-open.md`, `set-my-days/new-block-sheet/states/from-a-tapped-time.md`, `dev-notes/life-and-todos.md`.

- **Repeats, framing round 5 (Taylor, 2026-10-10; the New block sheet band, y 5040).** [PROPOSED] The repeat switch becomes a Repeats select with Google Calendar's behaviour as the baseline: Does not repeat, Daily, Weekly on <day>, Monthly on the <nth> <day>, Annually on <date>, Every weekday (Monday to Friday), Custom…. Custom: repeat every N days, weeks, months or years; repeat on chosen weekdays; monthly by date or by nth weekday; ends Never, On a date, or After N times. Default: weekly on its day when added from the usual week, Does not repeat from This week. A block stores one repeat rule (RFC 5545 RRULE, as Google does) instead of one copy per weekday, and shows on every day the rule hits in the usual week. [PROPOSED] Editing a repeating block asks This block, This and following, or All, as Google does; it extends D-USE-24's scoped ··· menu. [NEEDS DECISION] How monthly and yearly blocks show in a usual week that is seven weekdays (default: they appear in This week on their dates only, with a line in the usual week's day row). Awaiting Taylor. Carried: `set-my-days/new-block-sheet/states/repeats-menu.md`, `set-my-days/new-block-sheet/states/custom-recurrence.md` (D-USE-80, D-USE-87).

- **Set my days, step 9 round 4, second part (the Set my days band on "USE — set up", y 1260 to 2520, notes under each).** [PROPOSED] Overlapping blocks sit side by side at half width; the block just moved takes an ink edge and a line under the pair names the overlap with one fix ("Start at 8:57"); [ASSUMPTION] go mode runs the earlier block first. An event in no block becomes its own small plum block ("this Monday only"), with a Toaster and Undo. Tapping empty rail marks the slot (dashed ink, time tag in the rail) and opens New block there; [ASSUMPTION] it runs to the next block or 1 h. A usual-week edit names its reach in a Toaster ("This week's Thursday 15 follows, except blocks changed on that day", See it); [PROPOSED] a block changed for one date carries "Changed for this day", and Reset to usual puts it back. Loading: skeleton bands, everything else live, the + disabled. Error: RegionRetry under Orient, "The day didn't load." with Try again; chips still work. Offline: read-only, the StatusLine in the +'s place, bounds and ··· faded; [ASSUMPTION] blocks still open read-only. [PROPOSED] SaveStatus shows Saving, then Saved for 2 s, then nothing (earlier frames hold it); a failure reads "Not saved · Try again" in ink with the edit kept. Awaiting Taylor.
