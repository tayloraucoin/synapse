- **Life and To-dos, step 9 framing round 2 (Taylor's notes, 2026-10-10; same bands).** [PROPOSED] Chores and errands stay to-dos, but dedicated time for them is its own kind: a seventh kind, **To-dos**, named for what fills it rather than a place (Taylor: "Home" is location-specific and runs into exceptions, like errands out or an afternoon at Joey's). A To-dos block always pulls from the pool, filtered by where: Home (a three-hour Sunday reset: the bedroom, the pantry), Out (Saturday errands) or Anywhere. **Where** is a field on the to-do (Home, Out, Anywhere; Anywhere unless set [ASSUMPTION]), beside a new **Repeats** (a chore is a repeating to-do): the to-do sheet is drawn with both. **Life** keeps plans, social or not, and holds its own items like Wellness; it loses the From to-dos mode. Colours, Vesper's pick at Taylor's request: Life rose, To-dos moss (both existing tokens; yellow dropped for its clash with Break, Secondary and the warning). The model is on the canvas: a block is time, a to-do sits in one pool, a slot joins them for a date and is undone at the block's end if not done; an event pins inside whatever block its time falls in. Supersedes round 1's Life "From to-dos" and alternative (a)'s Home. Awaiting Taylor. Carried in part (the To-dos block in go mode): `day/now/states/todos-block.md`. Carried (the set-up half): `day/block-view/states/todos-sunday-reset.md`, `day/block-view/states/life-set.md`, `set-my-days/new-block-sheet/states/todos-kind.md`, `dev-notes/blocks-and-todos-model.md`, `dev-notes/how-we-got-here.md`.

- **Suggested icons** (Taylor, 2026-10-10; drawn at step 11). [PROPOSED] No separate "Suggest" button: the icon picker's Emoji tab opens with a "Suggested for <name>" row of up to five emoji, matched from the name's words against the emoji set's keywords on the device (emoji-mart style search), so it needs no model call and keeps Phase 1's no-AI rule (record 0001). With no name yet, the row reads "Add a name first to see suggestions." It applies wherever the `EmojiSlotButton` opens the picker: items, blocks, to-dos, meal topics. Carried: `interactions/icon-picker/index.md`, `interactions/icon-picker/states/emoji-suggested.md`, `interactions/icon-picker/states/no-name-yet.md`.
