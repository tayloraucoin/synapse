- **The work block, step 8 round 3 (Taylor's notes; now in the Work block band, y 16500 to 19020).** [PROPOSED] Status belongs to Linear-linked items only; a plain item keeps its checkbox, and making an item has no modes (linking it to Linear is what brings status). A linked item's box is its status shape, in the workspace's colour. Tapping the shape opens Taylor's workflow, grouped as in Linear:
  - Backlog: Backlog.
  - Unstarted: Blocked, Todo, Up next.
  - Started: In progress, Ongoing, Paused, In review, Changes requested.
  - Done, Canceled and Duplicate.

  Each status shows what it does here: In progress starts the timer; Ongoing and Backlog go back to the pool; Blocked, Paused and In review move to a Waiting group at the foot of the block; Done checks the item off; Canceled and Duplicate take it off today. Tapping the row opens the item, and long-press gains Status. [PROPOSED] With more than one Linear workspace, From Linear gets All plus one tab per workspace, and All groups the list by workspace. With one workspace connected there is no switch and no grouping, and the workspace name shows on an item only when there is more than one. [PROPOSED] Size is Linear's points with a one-word name (1 Quick, 2 Small, 3 Medium, 5 Large, 8 Huge), synced both ways. Picking a size sets a length range: Quick 15 to 30 min, Small 30 to 60 min, Medium 1 to 2 h, Large 2 to 4 h, Huge 4 h or more with "Bigger than a block. Split it?" [ASSUMPTION]. Length is Exact or Range, units switch on their own, and the day plans with the top of a range. [PROPOSED] Tier colours (a square beside the heading): Urgent red-500, Primary leaf, Secondary amber, Stretch sky. Events default to plum.
