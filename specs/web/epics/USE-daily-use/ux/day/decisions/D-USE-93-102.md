Ends D-USE-57's [PROPOSED]                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Taylor, thread 7 round 2 | 2026-10-12 |
| D-USE-98  | Three Week and pool defaults, as drawn or proposed. (1) Due badges: the warning tone from two days before the due date ("Due Thu", "Due tomorrow"), danger on the day and after ("Due today"); the date is Linear's due date, or Synapse's own on a plain item. (2) The pool sorts by Due date, then Priority, then client order (D-USE-96). (3) Week chips never show firing time; firing shows on Today only. Ends D-USE-41's and D-USE-42's [PROPOSED] on due states                                                                       | Taylor, thread 7 round 2 | 2026-10-12 |
