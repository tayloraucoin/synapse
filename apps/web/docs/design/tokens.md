# Synapse — tokens.md

One role per token:

- `paper` `ink` `surface` `hairline` `edge` `fill-muted`: surfaces; flip by theme.
- `text-body` `-secondary` `-muted` `-disabled`: text; `muted` large only.
- `accent-mark`, `ring`: this moment (D-P02); `accent-text` for its words.
- `accent` (bridge): hover surface only, not the mark (G-04).
- `violet-*`: off-schedule only. `destructive`: Delete account only.
- `chip-<hue>-bg/fg`: a category, always beside its name.
- `block-<kind>`: planning surfaces only; never `/today` or Schedule.

**Type:** `Text` variants; `review-*` per D-P01; `--fs-timer` the timer only.
**Space:** `--space-1`..`7`; widths `--content-text`, `--content-canvas`, `--measure`.
**Radius:** `--radius` controls, `--radius-sheet` sheets, `--radius-full` chips and dots.
**Elevation:** `--shadow-overlay` and `--scrim` only.
