- **D-P02 Verdigris is this moment** (tightens C-P05). `now` draws the now line, the current-item dot and the focus ring; `link` sets a few words; nothing is filled with it.
- **D-P03 Quiet wins** over Candid and Deliberate. No shadow; nothing round but the mark, a category dot and the current-item dot.
- Tabular minimum `caption` 12px; times and counts in `figure`. Warm neutrals, paper and ink (G-02). One density: `--row-min`, `--target`.
