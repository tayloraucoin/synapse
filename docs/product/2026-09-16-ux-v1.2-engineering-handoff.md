## 3. The doors, for Mason

Each with what I need, what I would take if you decline, and the cost of being wrong.

1. **`day_plans` as a table of references (B1, §11.5).** Need: a named, reusable composition of three templates, a work template, four times, training placements, breaks, fixture exclusions, and a weekday set unique per user. Decline path: none I would accept — a jsonb on `users` cannot be named and reused, and a `templates` row of kind `day` makes a day a block, which the model says it is not. Cost if wrong: one table nobody reads; the templates and the week build are untouched by its existence.
2. **Versions as jsonb on `habits` (L4, L5, §11.2).** Need: up to three named lengths, one default; the pick and the editor treat them as *one of*; the chosen one is snapshotted on the item. Alternative: a `habit_versions` table if the confirm service needs to address a version by id. Cost if wrong: a jsonb→table move later, small.
3. **Travel as two `day_items` (L7, §11.7).** Need: two rows the person can drop, shorten or move without touching the workout; the Schedule draws them as ends of one band. Alternative: `role` on the item instead of `parent_item_id`. Cost if wrong: the rows exist either way; only how the Schedule groups them changes.
4. **`quotes` as app content with a read policy (P6) and an admin tier (P7).** Need: a bank Taylor curates, readable by every signed-in person once published, never writable by them. The role is the first non-user-private surface in the app; the smallest honest version is an env allow-list read through `env.ts` and an `adminProcedure` tier that checks it. If you would rather not open the role this week: seed the table by migration, ship the opt-in switch, and log the surface as a follow-on. The design does not depend on the surface, only on the table.
5. **Tiptap into `@syn/ui` (P1).** Need: one editor composite with five controls and Markdown as the storage form. The boundary rule is the `frimousse` one: tiptap packages are `@syn/ui`'s only. The Markdown bridge is your pick; I have no preference beyond round-trip fidelity for the five controls. Cost if wrong: an editor swap behind a stable props contract.
6. **dnd-kit into `@syn/ui` (F5).** Need: one sortable composite, CC's convention. The existing `DragLayer` (schedule drag) stays; `SortableList` is for vertical lists only. Cost if wrong: none; it is the CC dependency already proven.
7. **`morning_mode` and the confirm-from-orient path (B6, B8).** Need: `Start the morning` under *set_from_plan* calls the confirm service with the plan's choices; R23's `original_scheduled_start` is written there as it is at the pick. Nothing detects; the tap confirms. Cost if wrong: the mode flips to *build* for everyone and the pick returns; one column.
8. **A passages bucket (P4).** Need: up to four images per passage through the signed-URL path with owner-segment checks. Reuse the `icons` grammar with a prefix or add a bucket; either is fine for the design.
9. **Cleanup migration scope (S6, P3, P9).** `earliest_wake_time`, `orient_passage`, `orient_show_last_night` stop being written; drop them in a later cleanup, never in the migration that adds their replacements — your own rule, restated so Reeve sizes it as a separate ticket.
