# Marketing changelog

**What this is:** a running log of what the marketing surface should change as the product changes, and the thinking around it. The landing page ([`landing-page-ux.md`](../ux/landing-page-ux.md)) is a work in progress that nobody is married to; rather than edit it every time the UX moves, entries accumulate here. When a UX version lands and Taylor decides the marketing should catch up, the open entries get executed against whatever the most current UX is, and marked done.
**Rule:** entries are appended, dated, and tied to the UX version that motivated them. Thoughts are welcome; nothing here is a decision until it's executed.

---

## Open

### 2026-09-11 · from UX v1.1 · Headline

**Change:** Lead with **"For when your schedule is as dynamic as you are."**
**Why:** It's what speaks to Taylor now and the line he'd get excited to read on a site. It names the audience the product is actually built for — the structure-seeking person whose days don't repeat — and it's honest about what the product does (bends the plan, records how it bent) without a single persuasion word.
**Notes:** Replaces whatever the current landing headline is. Sub-line should carry the honesty core in one sentence; the brand's "hosted, not sold" rule (v1 §9.1.3) still binds the page — no urgency, no streak language, no "join thousands".

### 2026-09-11 · from UX v1.1 · Audience framing

**Change:** The page should speak to one archetype — *I set my own structure, and it changes* — not to "anyone with habits."
**Why:** v1.1 builds for one archetype and greys the rest. Marketing to four audiences when one is live would over-promise.
**Notes:** Revisit when a second archetype is built (see [`phase-2-collection.md`](phase-2-collection.md) P2-16).

### 2026-09-11 · from UX v1.1 · What to show

**Change:** If the page shows the product, show the **morning quick-pick** and the **orient frame**, not the habit library.
**Why:** Those are the two surfaces that distinguish it from a habit tracker; the library looks like every other app.
**Notes:** Hypothesis, not evidence. Screens don't exist yet; log it so the page isn't built around the wrong surface later.
