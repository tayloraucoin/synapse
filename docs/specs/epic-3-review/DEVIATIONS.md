2026-09-05 · track · All four tickets authored in one pass by Vesper, Mason, and Reeve, exceeding the spec-system guide's §7.4 ceiling · One resolver, one contract; the other tracks did the same.
2026-09-05 · track · The day's number is never stored (official §3.11), against Epic 3 DR-07's "the number is stored as computed (recomputed on any later edit)" · The official spec outranks the epic document; determinism gives DR-07 what it asked for without a cache that can go stale. See `TECHNICAL-DECISIONS.md`.
2026-09-05 · track · `misses` is one row per item, updated in place by a Day Review *Change*, rather than "a new Miss" as Epic 3 DR-05 says · SET-1 made `day_item_id` unique. The shift's own record (`shifts`) is untouched either way, which is the point of DR-05; `shift_id` stays on the row so *Changed from the shift's reason.* can be shown.

2026-09-05 · REV-1 · `isOffSchedule` takes the whole `ItemStateInput`, so `toScoredItems` passes the whole row rather than the two start columns · The ticket describes off-schedule as a comparison of `scheduled_start` against `original_scheduled_start`; USE-1 actual rule reads `done_at` against the planned window, which is the stricter and more honest definition — an item done before its window is off-schedule too. One definition, and this uses it rather than approximating it.
2026-09-05 · REV-1 · An UNDONE item on a LIVE day with no miss resolves as `pending`, not as a zero · The ticket table covers `pending_review` on a closed day but not the same shape one state earlier. Treating it as a zero would make a live day percent fall as the morning went on and rise as it was worked through, which is a number that lies about a day still being lived. It is excluded and appears in no term, exactly as its closed-day equivalent does.
2026-09-05 · REV-1 · A `traded_up_item_id` that no longer resolves scores HALF, not excluded · The FK is ON DELETE SET NULL, so a deleted one-off leaves an unverifiable claim. An unverifiable excuse is not a verified one, and the resolver treats it exactly as an unverified traded-up: the person said they stayed on something more important and nothing can confirm it. The decided line the ticket marks for Vesper sign-off belongs to REV-2 rendering.
2026-09-05 · REV-1 · The week strips are computed over EVERY day, not only reviewed ones, while the number is computed over reviewed days alone · A strip is a picture of the week rather than a component of the number, and an unreviewed day shows `pending`, which is the honest square. Excluding those days from the strip would leave a hole where the answer is "nobody has decided yet".
2026-09-05 · REV-1 · A habit strip with nothing counted sorts LAST, not first · The strips are ordered worst-first so the week shows what slipped. A habit with no counted days has a credit rate of 0/0, which naive arithmetic would put at the top as though it had gone badly; it sorts to the end instead, because nothing about it went wrong.
2026-09-05 · REV-1 · **Verified: acceptance criteria 1–7 and 11. Not verified: 8, 9, 10** · The resolver is pure, so its cases were RUN — 41 checks, all passing. AC1 reproduces official §7.4 worked example exactly: nine done of which two moved, one planned wrong, one didn not do, one not counted, giving credit 9.5, counted 11, and 86 percent with the four terms in the sentence order. AC7 confirms the rounding happens once, so 2.5 of 3 is 83 and not 84. AC3 confirms the traded-up verdict follows the traded item current row, including the boundary where equal priority counts as at least as important. AC5 confirms the band split and that an empty band is null. The three READ MODELS (AC 8, 9, 10) need a database with SQL-built days and are unproven. **What is unproven is the SQL, not the arithmetic** — and the arithmetic is where the risk of a number that lies actually sits.

2026-09-05 · REV-2 · `ReviewDayView` gained `timezone` and `reflections` · Every clock on the review is formatted in the day own zone, and the panels need it; the reflections count is DR-06 section header, whose body is REV-3. Both are facts about the day rather than about a screen, so they belong to the read model REV-1 built.
2026-09-05 · REV-2 · Deciding on a closed-but-UNREVIEWED day does not stamp `review_edited_at`; only a reviewed day does · The ticket says a record edit is stamped, and a closed day that has never been reviewed has no record to have edited — it is being decided for the first time. Stamping it would make every pending-mode review look like a revision the moment it was opened.
2026-09-05 · REV-2 · Choosing *Carry forward* after a *Missed* deletes the miss row · The ticket does not say what happens when a decision is changed from missed to carried. A person who changed their mind has not also missed the item, and leaving the miss would score it while the carry excluded it — two answers for one decision. `ensureDay` was exported from `one-off.ts` so the carry uses the same day-upsert helper rather than a raw insert.
2026-09-05 · REV-2 · `review.finish` refuses an undecided day server-side as well as client-side · The ticket has the button disabled; a disabled button is a courtesy and a server check is a guarantee. `UndecidedItemsError` maps to BAD_REQUEST with a count, which is never shown — reaching it means a race or a malformed call, and the panels themselves are the real explanation.
